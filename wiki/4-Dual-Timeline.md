# Dual Timeline & Prophetic Chronology

This document details the chronological synchronization model, historical era divisions, and scriptural anchor verses implemented in **Shajara**.

---

## ⏳ Dual Calendar Synchronization

The timeline supports three display modes:
1. **Dual Synchronized (Default):** Displays both Hijri (BH / AH) and Historical (BCE / CE) dates side-by-side.
2. **Hijri Scale (BH / AH):** Focuses on Islamic historiographical chronology relative to the Hijra of 622 CE.
3. **Historical Scale (BCE / CE):** Focuses on archaeological and conventional historical estimates.

## 🏛️ The Seven Historical Eras

The prophetic timeline is categorized into seven distinct epochs:

1. **Dawn of Humankind (فجر البشرية والطوفان):**
   - Prophets: Adam, Idris, Nuh.
   - Core milestones: Creation of Adam, descent to earth, preaching of Idris, building of the Ark, the Great Deluge.

2. **Ancient Arab Prophets (الأنبياء العرب القدماء):**
   - Prophets: Hud, Salih.
   - Core milestones: Warning to the mighty civilization of ‘Ad at Iram, warning to Thamud at Al-Hijr, the Miracle of the She-Camel.

3. **The Patriarchal Era (عصر الخليل إبراهيم والآباء):**
   - Prophets: Ibrahim, Lut, Ismail, Ishaq, Yaqub, Yusuf.
   - Core milestones: The furnace of Nimrod, building of the Ka‘bah, running between Safa and Marwah, the test of sacrifice, the drama of Yusuf in Egypt.

4. **Exodus & Prophethood (عصر موسى والخروج):**
   - Prophets: Shu‘ayb, Musa, Harun.
   - Core milestones: Ten years at Madyan, the Burning Bush at Mount Tur, the Nine Signs to Pharaoh, the Parting of the Red Sea, the Revelation of the Torah.

5. **Kingdom of Israel (عصر الملوك وأنبياء بني إسرائيل):**
   - Prophets: Dawud, Sulayman, Ilyas, Al-Yasa‘, Yunus.
   - Core milestones: Victory over Goliath (Jalut), the Psalms (Zabur), dominion of Sulayman over winds and jinn, Yunus in the whale's belly.

6. **The Messianic Dawn (عصر زكريا ويحيى وعيسى):**
   - Prophets: Zakariya, Yahya, ‘Isa.
   - Core milestones: Maryam's sanctuary, miraculous birth of Yahya to elderly parents, virgin birth of ‘Isa, speech from the cradle, ascension to heaven.

7. **The Seal of Prophecy (عصر الرسالة المحمدية الخاتمة):**
   - Prophet: Muhammad ﷺ.
   - Core milestones: Year of the Elephant (570 CE), first revelation at Hira (610 CE), the Night Journey and Ascension (Isra and Mi‘raj), the Hijra to Medina (622 CE / 1 AH), Conquest of Mecca (8 AH), Farewell Pilgrimage and passing (11 AH / 632 CE).
